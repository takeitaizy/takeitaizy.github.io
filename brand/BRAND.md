# Take It AIZY: brand kit

Official logo files, approved by Oshar. **These are the only logo files to use**:
on the website, in proposals, specs, SOWs, decks, docs, email signatures and
anything else that goes out under the company name.

## Files

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `logo/take-it-aizy-logo-navy.svg` / `.png` | Default. Wordmark on white or light backgrounds |
| `logo/take-it-aizy-logo-white.svg` / `.png` | Wordmark on navy, dark or photo backgrounds |
| `logo/take-it-aizy-icon.svg` / `.png` | App icon, avatar, social profile, favicon source (navy rounded square, white Z) |
| `logo/take-it-aizy-mark-navy.svg` | The Z hourglass on its own, no background, for light surfaces |
| `logo/take-it-aizy-mark-white.svg` | The Z hourglass on its own, for dark surfaces |
| `favicon/` | Ready favicon set: `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, 16/32 PNG, `apple-touch-icon.png` (180), 192, 512 |

Format choice: **SVG** for the web. **PNG** (2400 px wide) for Word, Google Docs,
Gamma, Slides and PDFs, where SVG support is unreliable.

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Navy (logo, primary ink) | `#0E1C30` | Official, from the logo files |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Official |
| Light gray (logo sheet background) | `#DDDDDD` | Official, from the logo preview |
| Sand (accent, the hourglass "sand") | `#E8A33D` | Proposed for the website, not approved yet |

Sand is for fills and highlights only. It fails contrast as text on white.

## Rules

- The logo reads **TAKE IT AIZY**. In running text write **Take It AIZY**;
  use `takeitaizy` only for the domain (`takeitaizy.dev`) and handles.
- Only navy or white. No other colors, gradients, shadows, outlines or effects.
- Never stretch, rotate, crop or retype the wordmark in a font. Scale it proportionally.
- Clear space around the wordmark: at least the height of the "TAKE IT" line on every side.
- Minimum size: wordmark 120 px wide on screen / 30 mm in print; icon 16 px.
- On busy photos, use the white logo over a navy panel, not straight on the image.

## Placement in documents and proposals

- **Cover page:** navy wordmark, top of the page, about 35-45% of the page width.
- **Running header:** small navy wordmark (about 30 mm wide), aligned to the page's start edge.
- **Footer:** icon (about 8 mm) + `takeitaizy.dev` + `hello@takeitaizy.dev`.
- **Dark slide or cover:** white wordmark on navy `#0E1C30`.
- **Hebrew documents:** the wordmark stays in Latin letters, never transliterated.

## Fonts (proposed)

Heebo for Hebrew text; Montserrat for Latin labels and numbers (close to the
"TAKE IT" line in the logo). Not approved yet.
